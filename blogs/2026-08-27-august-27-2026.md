Feature General availability : You can reserve static external IPv6 addresses from bring your own IP addresses (BYOIP) sub-prefixes that are in EXTERNAL_IPV6_FORWARDING_RULE_CREATION mode. You can assign these addresses to forwarding rules for external passthrough Network Load Balancers and external protocol forwarding. You can also promote ephemeral IPv6 BYOIP addresses that are used by external forwarding rules to reserved static IP addresses.
