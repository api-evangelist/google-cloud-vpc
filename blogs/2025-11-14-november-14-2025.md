Feature Dynamic Private Service Connect interfaces are available in General Availability . Private Service Connect interfaces let service producers initiate connections to service consumers. You can update a VM instance to add or remove dynamic Private Service Connect interfaces without needing to restart or recreate the instance. For more information, see Private Service Connect interface types .
